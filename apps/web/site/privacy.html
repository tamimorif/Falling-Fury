Falling Fury — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Falling Fury is a simple arcade game. This policy explains, in plain language, what the game does (and does not do) with your information.

No personal data collected

Falling Fury does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no user profiles.

No analytics, ads, or trackers

The game contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers or SDKs.

What is stored on your device

The only data the game stores is your local high score, kept on your own device using localStorage. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear the game's local data or uninstall the app.

Works fully offline

The app works completely offline on iOS and Android. It does not require a network connection and does not send any data anywhere.

Children

Falling Fury is family-friendly and suitable for children. Because the game collects no personal data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

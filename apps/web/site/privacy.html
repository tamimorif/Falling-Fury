Falling Fury — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Falling Fury is a simple arcade game. This policy explains, in plain language, what the game does (and does not do) with your information.

Optional Game Center leaderboards

On iOS, you can choose to sign in to Apple Game Center. If you do, Falling Fury sends your high score to Apple so it can appear on the game's global leaderboard with your Game Center nickname. Other players can see leaderboard nicknames and scores. Apple manages your Game Center account and leaderboard data under Apple's privacy policy. You can play without signing in; Game Center features are not available in the browser or Android versions. Learn more in Apple's Game Center privacy notice.

No analytics, ads, or trackers

The game contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers or SDKs.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a local high score on your device using localStorage. On iOS, your best score can also be sent to Game Center when you sign in. The game does not operate its own player accounts or leaderboard servers.

Works fully offline

Core gameplay works offline on iOS and Android. An internet connection and Game Center sign-in are needed to view or submit iOS leaderboard scores.

Children

Game Center is optional. If used, Apple handles the account and leaderboard information according to its privacy practices and age settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

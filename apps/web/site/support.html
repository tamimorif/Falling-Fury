Falling Fury — Support

How to play

  • Tap the falling green targets to score a point each before they escape.
  • Red targets are dangerous — tapping them is worth extra points, but letting them hit the floor costs health.
  • Chain quick hits to build a combo, survive the waves, and beat your high score.

FAQ

There's no sound. What should I check?

First, make sure the silent switch (the ringer switch on the side of your iPhone) is off, and check that the volume is turned up. The game follows your device's sound settings.

Where is my high score saved?

Your high score is saved locally. On iOS, sign in to Game Center to submit your best score to the global leaderboard and see your rank. Game Center scores sync through Apple. Browser and Android play keep scores on the device.

How do I reset my high score?

Clearing the app's local data resets it — for example, delete and reinstall the app, or clear the site data for the game in your browser settings if you play in a browser.

Paste as plain text. Play renders line breaks, so the sections below display as written.

```
Falling Fury is a lightning-fast arcade tapper. Gems rain down, the arena never lets up, and every miss costs you. Tap the falling diamonds before they escape, chain combos, survive the surge, and chase one more run.

HOW TO PLAY

- Tap green gems to score a point and heal a little.
- Red danger gems are worth triple points — but if one slips past you, it hurts twice as much.
- Chain taps quickly to build combo multipliers (x2, x3, x4) and stack absurd scores.
- Miss a gem and you lose health. Run out of health and the run is over.
- Each wave speeds things up: faster falls, tighter spawns, rising pressure. How long can you last?

FEATURES

- One-thumb arcade action built for quick runs and "just one more" replays
- Combo multipliers that reward fast, risky tapping
- Red danger gems — big points, bigger consequences
- Endless wave-based difficulty that keeps ramping up
- Haptic feedback on hits and damage
- Smooth 60 fps canvas action with particles, floating score text, and screen shake
- Punchy chiptune-style sound effects — no headphones required
- High score saved right on your device, so you always know what to beat
- Fully offline — no account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases

WHY YOU'LL KEEP COMING BACK

Every run is different. Spawns are random, danger gems raise the stakes, and combos push you to tap faster than feels safe. Beat your high score, then beat it again.

FULLY OFFLINE

Falling Fury plays entirely on your device. No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No network connection required.

Got 30 seconds or 30 minutes? Falling Fury is pure pick-up-and-play arcade fury. Tap, combo, survive, repeat.
```
